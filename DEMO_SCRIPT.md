# Jaguar Demo Script

**Format:** Face cam (dark bg) → Screen share
**Target runtime:** ~3 minutes

---

## PART 1 — FACE CAM / DARK BG (recorded)

*Straight to camera. No intro fluff. Confident, unhurried.*

"Every minute, new tokens are launching on Solana.

Most of them are noise. A few of them are real setups — real liquidity, real volume, real momentum.

The problem is you have about two, maybe three minutes to tell the difference. By the time you've opened four tabs, checked the chart, looked at the pair on-chain, cross-referenced the volume — the window is already gone.

Traders who catch those windows consistently aren't faster. They have better signal.

This is Jaguar."

*[cut to screen share]*

---

## PART 2 — SCREEN SHARE: THE LIVE BOARD

*Open on the dashboard / launch board. Launches are ticking in live.*

"What you're looking at is a live feed of every new Solana pair being launched right now — scored in real time, as the data comes in.

Jaguar is connected to GoldRush's streaming API. Every new pair that hits the chain, every liquidity update, every volume tick — it comes through here.

Each launch gets a conviction score. Zero to a hundred. And a verdict — ignore, watch, or enter.

*(gesture at the board)*

These aren't static snapshots. The scores are moving. As the pair gets more data — more swaps, more volume, more price action — Jaguar updates the conviction in real time.

You can see pairs graduating from watch to enter as the signal builds. And you can see others fade out as momentum dies.

This is the whole board. But here's where it gets interesting."

---

## PART 3 — SCREEN SHARE: LAUNCH DETAIL + AI MEMO

*Click into Samantha ($Samantha) from the launch board.*

"Okay so this one is actually really interesting — Samantha.

*(let the score land)*

Score of 82, verdict enter, $444k in liquidity, nearly 5000 swaps in five minutes. On paper this thing looked live.

And Jaguar did flag it. All three personas — degen, momentum, risk-first — all got an enter signal. Paper trades opened across the board.

*(scroll to conviction delta chart)*

But look at the conviction delta. The 5m and 1h are building — but the 1m and 15m are flat at zero. Conviction isn't holding across the windows. That's the tell.

*(scroll to the memo)*

And the analyst memo caught it.

*(read the headline)*

"All three personas just blew up on this one — skip it."

It saw the $712k volume, the 137% price rip — and then it saw VOLATILITY_TOO_HIGH, the flat conviction windows, the classic pump structure. And it just said: walk away.

*(point at the timeline)*

And then the timeline confirmed it. Degen call failed. Momentum call failed. Risk-first call failed. All down 12% from entry.

That's the thing about Jaguar — it's not just telling you when to get in. It's tracking every call it makes, with real outcomes, and the AI is honest about when the setup was a trap.

Any tool can show you a green trade. This is what it looks like when the system actually protects you."

---

## PART 4 — SCREEN SHARE: PERSONA SWITCHER

*Switch to the persona selector — show degen / momentum / risk-first.*

"One more thing.

Not every trader has the same edge. A degen and a risk-first trader looking at the same setup should not be getting the same answer.

*(switch persona)*

Jaguar has three built-in trader personas. Same launch, same live data — watch what happens to the verdict when I switch.

*(flip from momentum → degen → risk-first, pause on each)*

Degen gets a bias toward early, thin setups. Risk-first penalizes volatility and thin liquidity harder. Momentum sits in the middle.

The score shifts. The verdict shifts. Because the same signal means different things depending on how you trade.

That's conviction that's actually calibrated to you."

*(short pause)*

"Jaguar is live at jaguaralpha.xyz.

Built on GoldRush, running on Solana. Real data, real decisions, in real time."


*[cut / end]*

---

## Tips for the Live Portion

- Have the dashboard open and warmed up before you start recording
- If a launch comes in hot during the screen share, chase it — that's your best moment
- For the memo, if one is already saved on a launch use it rather than generating live (avoids waiting)
- On the persona flip, go slowly — let the verdict label visibly change on screen before moving on
